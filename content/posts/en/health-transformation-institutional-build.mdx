> *This post is a translation of the original Arabic article.*

[**Health transformation**](https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/ar/explore/programs/health-sector-transformation-program) unfolds across an interconnected chain of steps: establishing clusters, setting up the [**Health Holding Company**](https://www.health.sa/ar/model-of-care), transferring responsibilities, mapping populations, building capabilities, changing the financing model, and then strengthening the independence of each cluster.

Each step builds the foundation for the next.

A cluster first needs to know which facilities and teams fall within its scope. Then it needs to understand the population it serves and what they need. After that, it builds its financial and operational systems, defines performance indicators, and develops its relationship with the funding and regulatory bodies.

This process transforms a collection of facilities into an accountable institution.

Institutional building requires [**governance**](https://www.health.sa/ar/model-of-care), leadership, data, financial systems, [**purchasing capabilities**](https://www.who.int/activities/promoting-strategic-purchasing), [**performance management**](https://www.health.sa/ar/moc-forum), and a culture that is oriented toward outcomes. It also requires ongoing clarity about where authority sits — between the Ministry, the Holding Company, the clusters, and the funders.

The speed of launching initiatives is one part of transformation. Sustaining them and making them work with independence and efficiency is the deeper part.

This is why transformation requires phased management. Each cluster advances according to its readiness, with clear criteria for moving from one phase to the next. Measurement focuses on the cluster's ability to manage its population, resources, care journeys, and outcomes.

[**Vision 2030 set the direction**](https://www.waelkabli.com/ar/blog/founders-note-vision-2030). Institutional building turns that direction into a way of working that holds every day.

The desired outcome is health clusters that possess decision-making authority, capability, and accountability — operating within an [**accountable care organization**](https://www.cms.gov/priorities-innovation-key-concepts-accountable-care-accountable-care-organizations) model and a nationally unified system. That is the difference between executing a program and building an institution.
